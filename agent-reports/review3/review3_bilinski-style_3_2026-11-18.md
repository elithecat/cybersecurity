## Summary

The advice half (A1 to C4) is close to her voice: imperatives, "you," brand names, short steps.
The first half and the 5 takeaway slides read like a written report: full sentences of 15 to 30 words,
no "I," and no question to the audience on any slide between the roadmap and "Questions?".
8 substantive findings, 9 minor findings.

Note on "we" and "I": CLAUDE.md 3.4 says "we" for the research in a talk. This talk has 1 speaker and no
collaborator slide, and her 2026 talk uses "I" for her own practice and judgment. I propose "I" only where
the slide states her own search, estimate, or judgment. If she prefers "we," keep "we" and ignore finding 2.
